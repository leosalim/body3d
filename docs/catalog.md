## 3. Cut out and synthesized

| Structure | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rectus abdominis | cut out of mesh `FJ1452` ("external oblique") along the linea semilunaris measured on the surface (`split.py`) | in BodyParts3D 4.0 the external-oblique mesh is the whole anterior wall, tendinous intersections of the rectus included |
| Latissimus dorsi | fibres stretched over a distance field of the trunk, fleshy origin along the erectors and the iliac crest, insertion in the intertubercular sulcus (`synth.py`) | absent from BodyParts3D 4.0 |
| Patellar ligament | flat band from the apex of the patella to the tibial tuberosity (`synth.py`) | absent from BodyParts3D 4.0; without it the patella floats |

## 4. Missing from BodyParts3D and not synthesized

Internal oblique and transversus abdominis, quadratus lumborum, multifidus, muscles of mastication and facial expression, thoracolumbar fascia. They are deep (or covered) in a training context; they enter the muscle-mass budget through the coverage factor (`morph.COVERAGE`).
